JUST ONE MORE CONNECTION

Every connection counts.

1

Find your color. Drag through two or more matching dots. Connect horizontally or vertically; step back to undo a dot.

2

Make it a loop. Return to an earlier dot to close a square or a bigger loop. Every dot of that color disappears.

3

Forge a Nova. In Rush, Daily, Zen, and Expedition, connect 5 or more dots without a loop. The last dot stays, falls with the board, and becomes a Nova. Choose where your next blast will begin. It needs room under the 2-Nova limit; ending on a Nova detonates it instead.

4

Get into a rhythm. Clear within 3.5 seconds of the board becoming playable to build a multiplier up to ×5. The rhythm bar shows your window; missing it lowers the multiplier one step at a time. Fill the fire meter for 10 seconds of double points. During Fever, a loop, Nova blast, or forged Nova adds 1 second per move, up to 3 extra seconds.

5

Follow the star. A Nova clears its 3×3 neighborhood, even other colors. A nearby Nova can trigger a chain reaction. Every 4 successful clears can also bring another, up to 2 on the board. Finish the mini mission for a flat score bonus and a fresh goal.

Rush and Daily start at 60 seconds. Each loop adds 2 seconds, up to 10 extra seconds per round. Three free shuffles help you find your next move. Zen has no clock. Expedition has 5 stages with a move budget: clear the dot target, choose an upgrade, and carry your board into the next stage. Each stage rewards a different kind of move. Expedition combos grow per move; upgrades can change Nova size and frequency.

Explore the Constellation Atlas. Each puzzle has a fixed board and refill sequence, a move budget, and a specific goal. Atlas keeps its original clearing rules: no forged Novas, timer, or shuffle. Finish for 1 permanent star; finish at par for 2, or at expert pace for 3. Replay to improve your best and unlock the next puzzle.

Your scores, stars, badges, and palettes stay in this browser. Active runs start fresh after a reload.

Keyboard friendly

Use arrow keys to move between dots. Enter or Space selects a dot and adds to your chain; Enter again on the last dot clears it. Escape cancels a chain. Outside the board, Space pauses and R starts a fresh run.
